What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list that organizes item links and comparison notes. Depending on its creator, it may include categories, seller details, listed prices, measurements and quality-control references. Always verify information against the current listing before ordering.

How do I use an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

Choose a category, open the current product listing, verify the available variants and measurements, and compare the full estimated cost. Save the item link and relevant notes before deciding whether to order.

Does a spreadsheet guarantee product quality or authenticity?

No. A product list, brand name or photograph does not independently establish authenticity, seller authorization or quality. Check the seller’s evidence, product details and applicable return terms.

What should I check in QC photos?

Check that the photographed item matches the ordered variant. Review visible condition, color, stitching, labels and any requested measurements. Photos cannot fully verify fit, durability, material composition or authenticity.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Use the individual seller’s size chart. Compare garment measurements with a similar item you own, and compare footwear guidance with your measured foot length. Size labels can differ between brands, sellers and styles.

Why can the final cost differ from the listed price?

The listed item price may exclude delivery, service fees, currency conversion costs or import charges. Shipping can also depend on destination, packaging, actual weight and volumetric weight. Obtain a current estimate before committing.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS discovery guide. It is not operated by or affiliated with Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès. Shopping links lead to CAFANS.
